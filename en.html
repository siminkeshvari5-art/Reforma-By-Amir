01

Renovating the home you live in

You'll keep living there while the work happens, so how it's run matters as much as how it ends.

When renovations go wrong, it's rarely one big mistake. The scope was agreed verbally and the price grew. The finish date passed and nobody explained why. The contractor became hard to reach. In the end, the quality depended on who happened to be on site that day.

We built the way we work to avoid exactly that:

  • A written scope: what's included, and what isn't.
  • A realistic programme, with updates at agreed points.
  • No extra work without your written approval, priced first.
  • One standard of care, whatever you spend.

05

How your renovation runs

Six steps, from your first message to handing your home back.

  1. Your request

    Tell us briefly what you have in mind. We reply within 24 hours by phone, WhatsApp or email, whichever you chose.

  2. Free visit

    We look at the space, check its condition and listen to what you want. No obligation.

  3. Itemised quote and written scope

    Materials, labour, taxes and our margin, what is and isn't included, and a realistic programme.

  4. Before work starts

    We agree the programme, payment stages and how we'll keep you updated. If your work needs a permit, we tell you which one and agree in writing who handles it.

  5. During the works

    Protection in place, daily clean-up, and updates at the agreed points. Any change is priced and approved by you in writing first.

  6. Finishing and handover

    We check every detail with you, complete any remaining points, clean properly and hand the home back ready to live in.

While we're in your home

  • Floors, doors and furniture covered before work begins
  • Sockets and switches protected
  • Tools and materials kept in agreed areas
  • Dust controlled and the site cleaned every working day
